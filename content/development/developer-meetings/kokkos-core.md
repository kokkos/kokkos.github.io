Overview
--------
The Kokkos Core developers meeting is intended to provide a platform for the
developers of [Kokkos Core]({{% ref "projects/kokkos-core" %}}) to meet and
discuss the needs of the library. The discussion typically focuses on new
feature design, implementation reviews, performance and portability concerns
across supported backends, and release planning.

Meetings
--------
* Schedule: on Wednesday at 11am ET (Eastern Time) / 9am MT (Mountain Time) /
  5pm CET (Central European Time), chosen to fit the time zones where most
  participants are located. The meeting follows US daylight saving time, so
  CET can be off by an hour for a few weeks each spring and fall while the US
  and Europe are on different clocks.
* Meeting link (Zoom) - See https://app.lfx.dev/projects/kokkos-project/calendar
* [Agenda and meeting minutes](https://github.com/kokkos/development/tree/main/meeting_notes/kokkos-core/dev-meeting)
