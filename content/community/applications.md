Kokkos is used in a wide range of codes, from large simulation frameworks to small libraries and research prototypes. This registry collects them in one place.

## Why a registry?

- **Advertise your work.** List your code so that others can find it. A project that needs a solution you already built can reach out and collaborate, instead of redeveloping the same thing again.
- **See what the community is doing.** We hear about many of the interesting things people do with Kokkos at the Kokkos User Group meetings. The registry lets us, and everyone else, see them all year round.
- **Show Kokkos' impact.** A visible record of who uses Kokkos, and for what, helps the project make the case to its sponsors for continued support.

This is a partial list, and we would like it to grow. Private and proprietary codes are welcome too: they can be listed as "Restricted". The "Status" column shows whether a project is evaluating Kokkos, in the middle of porting to it, or already using it in production runs.
