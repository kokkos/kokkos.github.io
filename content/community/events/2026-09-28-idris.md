The French laboratory IDRIS will host a 3 days formation session aimed at beginners.
The formation will be held from **September 28th to 30th**.

Inscription are still open and are free if your employer is either the CNRS or a french university.
To attend, registration is mandatory on IDRIS's website: http://www.idris.fr/docs/formations/hpc/formation-kokkos/

## Content

The following subjects will be presented during the formation:
- Kokkos basics
- Getting familiar with Kokkos
- Data containers
- Parallel loops
- Customisation of loops policy
- Parallel reductions
- Function annotation
- Atomic operations
- API of Kokkos' algorithms module
- SIMD vectorisation with Kokkos
- Debugging and profiling

## Target Audience

This formation will be given in French.

Basic knowledge of the following concept will be needed in order to follow:
- C++
- CMake
- Linux

This tutorial is not targeting fluent Kokkos users.
