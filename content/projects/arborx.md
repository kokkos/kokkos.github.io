ArborX is an open-source C++ library that brings fast geometric search to
modern HPC architectures. Built on Kokkos, it runs efficiently on multicore
CPUs and GPUs from multiple vendors, from a single node to distributed-memory
systems via MPI. ArborX provides spatial and nearest-neighbor searches over a
wide range of geometries, and uses them to power higher-level capabilities such
as density-based clustering (DBSCAN, HDBSCAN) and moving least squares
interpolation.
